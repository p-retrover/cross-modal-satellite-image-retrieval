Sensor guide

Sentinel-1 SAR: an active C-band radar transmits microwave pulses and records the backscattered echo. It operates day or night and through most cloud.

Sentinel-2: a passive multispectral imager measures reflected sunlight in visible, red-edge and near-infrared bands. Clouds and darkness limit acquisition.
